III. CPX Tokens, Utility & Transferability
3.1 Decentralized Exchanges
CPX Tokens may be transferred and traded through decentralized exchange (DEX) protocols that utilize automated market maker (AMM) liquidity pools administered by publicly available smart contracts. These protocols may incorporate standard smart contract features supporting liquidity management, transaction execution, protection against abusive or malicious trading activity, and the orderly operation of the underlying liquidity pools. Ciphex may maintain reserves of CPX Tokens and stable-value digital assets and may periodically contribute such assets to supported liquidity pools to facilitate transactional functionality, liquidity availability, and efficient protocol operation. Any such contributions are intended solely to support the operational functionality of decentralized trading infrastructure and should not be interpreted as market-making, price stabilization, trading activity, market manipulation, volume generation, or any representation regarding liquidity, market value, token performance, or future market activity.
3.2 Centralized Exchanges
Following the availability of CPX Tokens through a decentralized exchange, CPX Tokens may become eligible for trading on one or more centralized exchanges (CEX), subject to applicable exchange-specific requirements and eligibility. Any decision to make CPX Tokens available for trading is determined solely by the applicable exchange in accordance with its independent listing standards, commercial considerations, and operational procedures. If listed on one or more centralized exchanges, CPX Tokens may continue to trade simultaneously on decentralized exchanges and other compatible trading venues where supported. Any promotional campaigns, staking services, reward programs, trading competitions, airdrops, earn products, savings products, referral campaigns, market development initiatives, liquidity incentive programs, or similar offerings made available by a centralized exchange in connection with providing initial listing promotions for CPX Tokens are administered independently by the applicable exchange. Ciphex does not control, administer, sponsor, guarantee, or assume responsibility for the availability, continuation, performance, or outcomes of any exchange-sponsored program.
3.3 Ecosystem Liquidity Initiatives
Liquidity-related activities, including liquidity staking, liquidity aggregation, automated pool balancing, liquidity management services, related token utility incentives, and similar programs, operate independently from the protocol-level function of decentralized exchanges, centralized exchanges, automated market makers (AMM), and other trading venues, and do not constitute discretionary management of secondary market trading activity. Ciphex may utilize independent third-party infrastructure providers, smart contract services, vault systems, liquidity-balancing services, liquidity-staking protocols, or similar services to support the operational administration, technical maintenance, liquidity functionality, and Ecosystem utility of CPX Tokens. Liquidity-staking services may permit eligible participants to deposit CPX Tokens or liquidity positions into protocol-based programs that provide utility-based incentives, Ecosystem rewards, or other program benefits, subject to the applicable program terms and conditions. Any such services operate according to predetermined rules, are publicly verifiable, and do not influence market prices, trading activity, trading volume, liquidity levels, or price discovery.
3.4 Exchange Independence
Ciphex does not own, operate, control, host, administer, influence, or govern any decentralized exchange, centralized exchange, automated market maker, trading venue, or exchange-operated reward program, and does not exercise discretionary authority over trade execution, trade matching, order routing, market pricing, liquidity provisioning decisions, or exchange operations. Participation by liquidity providers, tokenholders, users, and other Ecosystem participants is voluntary. The availability of liquidity staking services, utility programs, rewards, or other Ecosystem benefits may vary over time based upon the independent decision of exchanges, protocol operators, third-party service providers, market participants, applicable protocol mechanisms, and prevailing market conditions. Ciphex makes no representation or warranty that CPX Tokens will be listed, remain listed, achieve any particular level of trading activity, price or liquidity, or be continuously available for trading on any exchange or trading venue.
3.5 Ecosystem Utility Incentives
Ciphex may support Ecosystem liquidity initiatives, liquidity staking services, token utility incentives, points programs, referral programs, Ecosystem participation rewards, and other utility-based initiatives through independent third-party service providers, decentralized protocols, or proprietary Ecosystem services. Any such programs are intended to promote Ecosystem awareness, accessibility, participation, and utility, and should not be interpreted as investment programs, market support mechanisms, or guarantees of economic performance or future outcomes.
3.6 No Representation on Performance
The existence of any liquidity program, staking service, exchange promotion, exchange listing, liquidity pool, rewards program, airdrop, referral program, earn program, or similar activity should not be interpreted as a representation, warranty, or assurance regarding the value, liquidity, availability, trading volume, price stability, market performance, or future trading activity of CPX Tokens.

Except to the extent required by applicable law, Ciphex Services does not hold itself out as operating a securities exchange, alternative trading system, broker-dealer, investment adviser, marketplace, crypto-asset trading platform, or any other regulated trading venue, intermediary, or financial services business requiring registration, licensing, authorization, or regulatory approval.
3.8 Independent Secondary Market Activity
Any secondary market activity involving CPX Tokens or tokens developed for enterprise tokenization projects, including trading, liquidity provisioning, liquidity staking, liquidity aggregation, automated pool balancing, exchange listings, rewards programs, staking programs, trading competitions, airdrops, earn programs, referral programs, or similar activities, occurs independently of Ciphex and outside its control. Ciphex may utilize independent third-party services to support Ecosystem functions associated with CPX Tokens or tokens developed through enterprise tokenization projects.
