Growth & Expansion RFP Deadline  
 
CipheX confirms that its Services RFP, which invited experienced professionals to submit proposals in 
support of priority initiatives including CPX token listing preparation, financing strategy development, digital 
market awareness and communications strategy, and operational and technical scaling, has formally 
closed in accordance with the RFP guidelines. CipheX received a strong volume of qualified proposals 
across each of the specified service categories.  At the request of several respondents seeking add itional 
time to supplement their submitted proposals, CipheX has granted a limited extension.  
 
During this extension period, existing respondents may submit amendments or supplemental materials to 
their original proposals. This opportunity applies uniformly to all current respondents. No new proposals 
will be accepted or reviewed during the extensio n period.  The revised deadline for submission of 
amendments or supplemental materials is 6:00 PM EST on March 4, 2026. This extension does not delay 
CipheX’s ongoing review of proposals already received. Follow up correspondence and proposal 
discussions are expected to commence this week. In accordance with the Services RFP guidelines, final 
selection determinations are anticipated to occur as part of the structured follow up and discussion 
process. 
 
CipheX Alpha Reduces AI Functionality 
Since November 2025, CipheX Alpha has materially reduced its reliance on artificial intelligence 
components, decreasing certain AI dependencies by nearly 80 percent. This reflects a deliberate 
architectural decision rather than a reduction in planned capab ility. As Phase III optimization progresses 
across risk, execution, and capital management layers, the system continues refining deterministic 
execution logic and strengthening rule -based controls. The objective of this recalibration is to ensure 
system be havior remains interpretable, testable, and reproducible under varied market conditions.  AI 
functionality has been retained only within limited backend analytical modules where statistical, pattern 
recognition, and other macro economic information  provides incremental value. These components 
operate under defined constraints and are subject to peer review by experienced Abacus contributors, 
reinforcing validation standards and limiting model drift. By narrowing AI usage and strengthening 
architectural oversight, the system continues improving target identification accuracy, execution timing, 
and layered risk controls, supporting reduced loss exposure and consistent capital preservation disciplines. 
System refinement and performance calibration remain active priorities as development advances toward 
initial commercial release. Interim or quarterly optimization updates may be issued as material milestones 
are achieved.
