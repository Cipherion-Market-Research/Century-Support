ECOSYSTEM UPDATE

New Crypto Regulations 
The introduction of new crypto regulations presents significant benefits for CipheX, the Alpha CPX 
Network, and its planned expansion into real -world services. Clearer regulatory frameworks will 
allow CipheX to reinforce institutional credibility and attra ct higher-tier participants as it advances 
its transformation toward regulatory compliance, institutional -grade governance, and enhanced 
financial transparency across its Ecosystem. These developments will ultimately enhance trust and 
accessibility for Alpha CPX' s AI-driven portfolio management systems among everyday consumers 
and future institutional investors, lay ing a solid foundation for the secure integration of tokenized 
real-world assets and broader market participation. 
CipheX Token Lockups and Monthly Vesting 
The completion of certain CPX Token lockups is just days away. This week, the earned portion of 
unrestricted CPX Tokens, derived from each token holder' s principal balance of restricted CPX  
Tokens, will become claimable. Once claimed, these tokens can be transferred directly to personal 
digital wallets. After the lockup period ends, the principal balance of restricted CPX Tokens will 
automatically transition into its twelve-month vesting schedule. Under this vesting schedule, token 
holders may claim an equal portion of their principal CPX Tokens at the end of each month and 
transfer those tokens to their wallets. Once claimed, these CPX Tokens will become unrestricted 
and tradable upon CipheX's planned listing on the Uniswap decentralized exchange (DEX). 
CipheX Token Claiming and Presale 
The CipheX Token Claiming Dashboard is scheduled to open on or before July 31, 2025. Once live, 
token holders will be able to connect their wallets to claim both their earned CPX Tokens from 
completed lockups and their ongoing monthly vesting allocations. The current CipheX limited 
presale section will transition into the dedicated access point for the Token Claiming Dashboard. 
Upon this transition, the limited presale will officially close and no longer be accessible to the 
general public. CipheX may continue certain financing activities after the  limited presale. However, 
any future financing activities  will be restricted to accredited and qualified investors, following  
applicable securities regulations. Additional information can be found on the limited presale section 
of the CipheX website. 
Active Trading of CipheX Tokens 
Currently, CipheX Tokens (CPX) are not listed for trading on any decentralized exchange, and no 
active market exists for thes e tokens. Any unrestricted CPX Tokens claimed through lockup 
completion or monthly vesting will remain untradable until CipheX finalizes its planned listing on the 
Uniswap decentralized exchange (DEX).  CipheX is currently targeting its initial DEX listing for 
September 2025. This timeline is provisional and subject to change.
