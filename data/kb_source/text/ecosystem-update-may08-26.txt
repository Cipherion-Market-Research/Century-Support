Ecosystem Update 
May 08, 2026 
 
Exchange Listing and Go-to-Market Execution 
CipheX has engaged Verdicti Ventures  to support its CPX token listing strategy, financing readiness, and broader go-
to-market execution, marking an important step in CipheX 's transition toward broader market access, token liquidity 
development, and commercial growth for its  CipheX ecosystem which is comprised of its Abigail Execution 
Management System (EMS), Abigail Asset Management System (AMS), and Altas RWA Capital Services (Atlas), which 
together form the foundation of its digital asset execution, asset management, and real -world asset tok enization 
infrastructure. 
Over the first 120 days, Verdicti Ventures will focus on advancing CPX Token listings across both decentralized and 
centralized exchanges, expanding CipheX 's international exchange network, and supporting early market access, 
exchange readiness, and responsible liquidity development across key cryptocurrency markets. In parallel with 
exchange execution, Verdicti will support strategic financing and partnership initiatives to strengthen CipheX 's 
operational capacity, expand development resources, establish key commercial partnerships, and increase market 
awareness of the broader CipheX ecosystem as its services move closer to commercialization. 
Kevin O 'Brien, founder of Verdicti Ventures,  joins CipheX as an expert contributor and member of the CipheX 
leadership team, focusing on executing these strategic initiatives. With more than 17 years of crypto -native venture 
capital and go- to-market experience, Kevin brings established relationships across institutional investors, 
cryptocurrency exchange networks, and strategic partners throughout North America, Europe, and Asia. His 
experience and international network strengthen CipheX's market positioning as it advances toward exchange listings, 
commercial partnerships, and broader ecosystem growth. Additional updates on these initiatives will be provided as 
key milestones are achieved. 
CipheX Alpha Phase III Optimization Summary 
Phase III optimization of CipheX Alpha continues to demonstrate measurable progress. As of April 30, the system 
recorded a 2.69% period -end return, an improvement of approximately 1.67% over the March 31 result. This 
performance reflects continued enhancem ents in key optimization areas, including adaptive risk management, loss 
containment, and asset selection across cryptocurrency markets. Further development details are available in the 
Abacus I AMS/EMS Development Roadmap and the March 31, 2026, Q1 Optimization Results, both published on the 
CipheX website. Live market activity updates remain available through the CipheX Alpha section. System refinement 
and performance calibration remain active priorities as development progresses toward its initial commercial release, 
with interim and quarterly optimization updates expected as major milestones are reached.
