Ecosystem Update 
Published December 31, 2025 
 
CipheX Alpha (Formerly Alpha Centurion Network) 
 
CipheX completed development of the CipheX Asset Management System (CipheX Alpha) during the second 
half of 2025 and initiated live market trading to validate proof of concept and support continuous system 
refinement. Since entering live market conditions in late May, CipheX Alpha has delivered strong performance 
through year -end, demonstrating operational resilience, execution consistency, and reliability in real 
markets. CipheX will publish these initial test results on the CipheX platform in January for reference, 
including opening its view-only dashboard to see live market performance of the CipheX Alpha system. 
Ongoing enhancements will continue as CipheX advances toward commercial deployment , targeted 
sometime in 2026. This next phase is being structured with regulatory assurance as a core design principle, 
aligning the CipheX Alpha Ecosystem with current and anticipated regulatory frameworks governing 
autonomous portfolio management platforms. This disciplined approach positions CipheX for scalable 
adoption, institutional credibility, and long-term relevance within an evolving global marketplace. 
Preparation for Market Trading of CipheX Tokens 
CipheX will begin preparing its CipheX Tokens for open market trading on or before the end of March 2026. All 
related platform enhancements, marketing expansion, token supply adjustments, and financing are part of 
the coordinated preparation for open market trading. Beginning in January and continuing through the 
anticipated market opening, CipheX will implement phased improvements to the CipheX platform alongside 
increased marketing and promotional activity to strengthen market readiness and commercial vis ibility. In 
addition, CipheX will automatically execute its planned token burn effective January 1, 2026, reducing excess  
supply by approximately 32%, with updated tokenomics published on the CipheX site in early January. 
Financing during this preparation period will be provided internally, with any subsequent financing structured 
to comply with applicable regulatory frameworks, thereby ensuring a disciplined, compliant transition into 
open-market trading.

About The CipheX Ecosystem 
 
CipheX is a decentralized ecosystem providing access to the Alpha CPX Network (Alpha CPX), a fully 
autonomous AI portfolio management platform delivering institutional -grade performance to everyday 
consumers. Designed to operate without intermediaries, Alp ha CPX gives users full control of their digital 
assets while enabling advanced market execution, strategy automation, and portfolio performance 
optimization.
