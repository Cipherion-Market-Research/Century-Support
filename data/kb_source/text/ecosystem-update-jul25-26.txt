ABOUT CIPHEX CAPITAL ECOSYSTEM 
 
The Ciphex Capital Ecosystem ( or "Ciphex") is a n ecosystem focused on emerging enterprise tokenization and 
autonomous market systems, reducing complexity and improving efficiency across capital formation, 
commercialization, and market participation as these markets evolve into intelligent digital capital markets. The 
ecosystem comprises two primary products and services: Atlas RWA Services (or "Atlas"), an enterprise tokenization 
system focused on efficient capital formation, information transparency, and project lifecycle management, reducing 
traditional inefficiencies while increasing market accessibility and participation; and Ciphex Alpha ( or "Alpha"), an 
autonomous market system focused on evolving digital capital markets, reducing user complexity while increasing 
conﬁdence through consistent market execution, intelligent risk oversight, and disciplined portfolio management.
