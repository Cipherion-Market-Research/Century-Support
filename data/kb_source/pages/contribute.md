Introduction

Global capital markets are undergoing a sustained structural transformation driven by enterprise tokenization, artificial intelligence, digital financial infrastructure, and technology-enabled investment services. This evolution is supported by unprecedented investment in digital infrastructure. Gartner estimates global information technology spending will reach approximately US$5.6 trillion during 2025, while IDC projects global digital transformation spending will exceed

US$4 trillion

by 2027. As these markets continue to evolve, commercial opportunity is increasingly shifting beyond technological innovation toward business models that improve capital formation, investment management, market accessibility, and commercial connectivity across traditional and digital capital markets.

‘Boston Consulting Group estimates that the global tokenized asset market could expand to nearly

US$19 trillion by 2033

’

Industry research reinforces the scale of this transformation. Boston Consulting Group estimates the global tokenized asset market could expand from approximately US$600 billion in 2025 to nearly

US$19 trillion

by 2033, representing a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of approximately 54%. At the same time, PwC projects global assets under management will increase from approximately US$139 trillion in 2024 to approximately

US$200 trillion

by 2030, reflecting the continued expansion of institutional and private capital markets. As digital capital markets mature, enterprise financing, investment management, digital capital formation, and supporting market infrastructure are becoming increasingly interconnected. Commercial success will depend not only on technological innovation but on the ability to transform emerging technologies into practical products, scalable commercial services, and sustainable revenue-generating activities.

1. Purpose of Contributions

The Ciphex Contribution Program (

“Contributions”

or the

“Contribution Program”

) is administered following the terms and conditions described in this section. Eligible participants may contribute toward the initial market entry and commercial monetization of the Ecosystem. The Contribution Program is intended to support the initial commercial release of Atlas and Alpha, together with Ecosystem initiatives including, without limitation, blockchain infrastructure and protocols, technical security enhancements, token utility development, liquidity initiatives, commercial partnerships, community engagement and targeted go-to-market activities focused on commercial engagement, product adoption, Ecosystem participation, and revenue generation (collectively, the

“Ciphex Expansion”

).

2. Contribution Stages

The Contribution Program is separated into three stages

(“Contribution Stages”)

and begins on a date determined by Ciphex, marking the opening of the Contribution Program and its first stage. Each Contribution Stage remains open for up to 30 calendar days

(“Active Days”)

from its commencement date unless fully allocated before the end of its Active Days. When the Active Days of a Contribution Stage end, no further Contributions may be made, and the next Contribution Stage immediately begins with its applicable minimum Contribution. The Active Days are governed by a smart contract created specifically to administer the terms, conditions, and functions of the Contribution Program (“Smart Contract”). Ciphex has the authority, at its sole discretion, to end any Contribution Stage before the end of its Active Days. Ciphex cannot modify the Smart Contract to pause or extend the Active Days of any Contribution Stage or reopen a Contribution Stage once its Active Days have ended, or if ended earlier by Ciphex.

The exchange value of each Ciphex token remains fixed

at $0.20 (USD)

throughout the Contribution Program. The maximum Contribution permitted during any Contribution Stage is 100,000 Ciphex tokens or the equivalent exchange value of $20,000 (USD), subject to applicable exchange rates (“Maximum Contribution”). The final exchange value of any Contribution may vary depending on the selected method of exchange. Ciphex has no control over the exchange rates applied by available methods of exchange when a Contribution is made. The Contribution Program ends upon completion of the Active Days of the Final Stage, unless ended earlier by Ciphex. The Contribution Program and any Contribution Stage are not subject to a minimum Contribution threshold, and Contributions become immediately available for allocation toward the Ciphex Expansion.

3. CPX Token Allocations

The Ciphex tokenomics follows a deflationary supply model. This model permanently disables Ciphex from producing additional tokens

(“CPX Tokens”)

to increase its available token supply. All Ciphex tokens allocated to the Contribution Program are sourced exclusively from existing CPX Treasury Reserves

(“CPX Reserves”)

. A total of 70,000,000 CPX Tokens have been allocated from CPX Reserves to an assigned Ciphex wallet created specifically for the Contribution Program

(“Contribution Reserves”)

.

The Contribution Reserves represent approximately 19.42% of existing CPX Reserves. If fully allocated during the Contribution Program, the Contribution Reserves will represent approximately 6.87% of the current fully diluted CPX Token supply. Based on the fixed exchange value of $0.20 (USD) per CPX Token, an implied fully diluted ecosystem valuation is approximately $203.71M (USD). If the Contribution Reserves are not fully allocated by the end of the Final Stage

(“Final Completion”)

, any remaining CPX Tokens in the Contribution Reserves are returned to CPX Reserves. The operational and treasury management policies of Ciphex govern the CPX Reserves. Additional information is available in the

2026 Ciphex Tokenomics Overview

in the

section of this website.

5. Lockup and Vesting

CPX Tokens allocated to the Contribution Program are subject to a

6 (six) month

restriction period consisting of a 3 (three) month (90-day) lockup period (

“Lockup”

) followed by a 3 (three) month (90-day) vesting period (

“Vesting”

). The Lockup begins on the date a Contribution is made. During the Lockup, CPX Tokens cannot be claimed, transferred, sold, pledged, assigned, or otherwise disposed of. Once the Lockup period ends, Vesting immediately begins in 3 (three) equal monthly installments, available for claim at the end of each month until all available CPX Tokens are fully vested. Lockup periods and Vesting schedules are governed by the Smart Contract of this Contributions Program and cannot be accelerated, shortened, waived, or otherwise modified. Contributors may view their CPX Token balance, Lockup period, Vesting schedule, and available claim balance through the Token Claim Center of this website. Contributors are responsible for monitoring and claiming their CPX Tokens.

7. Participation and Claiming

Eligible participants may contribute only through the contribution panel

(“Contribute Panel”)

at the top of this section. Participants may contribute multiple times during any Contribution stage, with each Contribution ranging from the applicable minimum up to the Maximum Contribution limit. If, during the Early Stage or Growth Stage, a Contribution exceeds the remaining CPX Tokens allocated to that stage, then: (a) the remaining CPX Tokens are allocated from that stage; and (b) the balance is allocated from the next available Contribution Stage. If the same circumstances occur during the Final Stage, only the remaining CPX Tokens allocated to the Final Stage will be exchanged, and any remaining portion of the desired Contribution will not be completed. CPX Tokens may be obtained only through the available exchange options provided in the Contribute Panel. Contributors may view their CPX Token balance, Lockup period, Vesting schedule, and available claim balance through the Token Claim Center of this website. Contributors are responsible for monitoring and claiming their CPX Tokens.
