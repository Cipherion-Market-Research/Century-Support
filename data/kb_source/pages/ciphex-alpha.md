Operating model

How Abigail

operates.

Validation

Every position is the result of statistical validation of price dynamics, liquidity flow, and market conditions, not a forecast, not a signal, not a bet. A disciplined, probability-driven process applied 5,800+ times a day.

Adaptive

When markets shift, she doesn't wait for stop-losses to trigger. She reads where capital is actually moving and adjusts, reducing exposure, shifting defensively, and containing drawdowns before they compound.

Monitors 24/7

The system monitors major market movements and every shift in conditions across traditional and digital markets, 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.
