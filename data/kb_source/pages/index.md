Transformation of Financial Markets

Market Transformation

Financial institutions are investing heavily in digital technology financial services and infrastructure. Gartner estimates worldwide information technology spending will reach approximately US$5.6 trillion in 2025, while IDC projects global digital transformation spending will exceed US$4 trillion by 2027, reinforcing modernization across capital markets.

Self-Directed Investing

PwC projects global assets under management will increase from approximately US$139 trillion in 2024 to approximately US$200 trillion by 2030, driven by expanding investor participation, technology-enabled investment platforms, and broader access to self-directed investing, reinforcing continued growth across institutional and retail capital markets.

Enterprise Tokenization

Boston Consulting Group estimates the tokenized asset market could expand from approximately US$600 billion in 2025 to nearly US$19 trillion by 2033, representing an estimated 54% compound annual growth rate (CAGR), reinforcing continued institutional adoption and expanding commercial opportunities across digital capital markets.
