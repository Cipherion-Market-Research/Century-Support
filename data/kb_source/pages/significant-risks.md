Significant Risks

Date: July 18, 2026

V. Significant Risks

Ciphex is an early-stage digital asset Ecosystem operating within emerging digital asset markets that involve significant legal, regulatory, technological, operational, cybersecurity, market, liquidity, and commercial risks. These markets include digital assets, tokenized real-world assets, cryptocurrency markets, decentralized protocols, prediction-based markets, and other emerging digital financial technologies. Any person accessing, acquiring, holding, using, or participating in Ciphex products, services, platforms, protocols, Digital Financings, or CPX Tokens should carefully review and understand these disclosures before participating and accept full responsibility for their participation, decisions, and associated risks.

5.1 Digital Asset Risk

Participation in digital asset, crypto-asset, and virtual asset activities involves significant risk, including the possible loss of all capital, digital assets, tokens, fees, or other assets committed or utilized in connection with any Ciphex product, service, platform, protocol, Digital Financing, or transaction. Market volatility, technological failures, cybersecurity incidents, smart contract vulnerabilities, liquidity constraints, operational failures, regulatory developments, and other unforeseen events may adversely affect the value, availability, functionality, or transferability of digital assets, resulting in partial or total loss. Participants should not access, acquire, hold, use, or participate in CPX Tokens or any Ciphex products or services unless they fully understand and are willing to accept these risks.

5.2 User Responsibility for Legal and Regulatory Compliance

Ciphex makes no representation, warranty, or assurance that any product, service, feature, platform, protocol, CPX Token, Digital Financing, or related activity will be available, permitted, or compliant in any particular jurisdiction. The availability of any Ciphex products, services, Digital Financings, or related activities may be restricted, conditioned, suspended, or prohibited based on applicable laws and regulations, governmental actions, licensing requirements, sanctions programs, anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing requirements, securities laws, financial services regulations, consumer protection requirements, tax obligations, or other legal considerations.

5.3 No Legal, Tax, Financial, or Regulatory Advice

Ciphex does not provide legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, financial, investment, or compliance advice. No information, communication, publication, website content, marketing material, report, presentation, or other material made available by Ciphex should be relied upon as legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, financial, investment, or other professional advice. Persons should obtain independent professional advice appropriate to their individual circumstances.

5.4 Participant Representations and Assumption of Risk

By accessing, acquiring, purchasing, holding, using, participating in, or otherwise interacting with any Ciphex product, service, platform, protocol, Financing, or CPX Tokens, each person represents and warrants that they have reviewed and understood the disclosures, warnings, risk disclosures, legal notices, and other materials made available by Ciphex. Each person further represents and warrants that they have independently assessed the legal and regulatory requirements applicable within their jurisdiction and are solely responsible for determining whether their participation is lawful and appropriate. By participating, each person knowingly acknowledges and accepts all risks associated with such activities, including the possible partial or total loss of capital, digital assets, CPX Tokens, access rights, or other economic value.

5.5 Macroeconomic and Intrinsic Risk

The Ciphex Ecosystem, including CPX Tokens, Digital Financings, and any current or future products, services, platforms, protocols, or autonomous systems, may be materially affected by adverse macroeconomic and financial conditions. Changes in interest rates, monetary policy, inflation, currency valuations, sovereign debt conditions, fiscal policy, global liquidity cycles, credit availability, and capital flows may adversely affect the value, liquidity, volatility, and market conditions of digital assets, tokenized assets, cryptocurrencies, and traditional financial assets. There can be no assurance that Ciphex or any current or future products, services, platforms, utility features, or CPX Tokens will successfully anticipate, adapt to, mitigate, or perform favorably during adverse macroeconomic or financial conditions.

5.6 Geo-Economic and Geopolitical Risk

Economic disruptions, including recessions, banking sector instability, credit contractions, geopolitical conflicts, trade restrictions, sanctions programs, energy market disruptions, public health emergencies, natural disasters, or systemic financial events, may increase market volatility, reduce liquidity, impair price discovery, and contribute to significant market dislocations or prolonged adverse market conditions. Such events may adversely affect participants, counterparties, service providers, digital asset markets, and the broader financial system. There can be no assurance that Ciphex, CPX Tokens, Digital Financings, or any current or future products, services, platforms, protocols, or autonomous systems will remain unaffected by global economic, geopolitical, regulatory, or financial developments or that such events will not materially and adversely affect Ecosystem activities, operations, market participation, or user outcomes.

5.7 Quantitative and Algorithmic Modeling Risk

There can be no assurance that any current or future Ciphex product, service, platform, protocol, autonomous system, analytical model, algorithm, probability framework, artificial intelligence component, or other analytical methodology will successfully identify, anticipate, adapt to, or perform effectively under all market conditions. Financial markets may change rapidly, unexpectedly, or in ways that historical data, quantitative models, simulations, probability frameworks, or automated systems may not accurately reflect or anticipate.

Accordingly, there can be no assurance that any analytical model, algorithm, probability framework, autonomous system, artificial intelligence component, or other methodology utilized by Ciphex will generate accurate outputs, favorable outcomes, profitable results, or effective risk management under actual market conditions.

5.8 Market Infrastructure and Execution Risk

Autonomous trading, analytical, execution, tokenization, and other Ecosystem activities may be adversely affected by disruptions involving market data providers, exchanges, blockchain networks, validators, liquidity providers, custodians, technology infrastructure, communication networks, regulatory actions, market closures, trading restrictions, exchange failures, cybersecurity incidents, or other events affecting market access, execution quality, pricing accuracy, operational continuity, or transaction settlement. There can be no assurance that any market data source, third-party exchange or related services, blockchain network, or infrastructure component, technology provider, third-party service provider, or execution venue utilized by Ciphex will remain continuously available, operate without interruption, or perform as expected. Such disruptions may result in delays, errors, reduced functionality, financial losses, or the partial or complete inability to access certain products, services, Digital Financings, or Ecosystem activities.

5.9 Significant Market Volatility Risk

Digital assets, cryptocurrencies, tokenized assets, and related financial markets are highly volatile and may experience rapid and substantial price fluctuations over short periods. Volatility may result from macroeconomic developments, monetary policy changes, regulatory actions, technological failures, cybersecurity incidents, exchange disruptions, concentrated trading activity, liquidity shortages, changes in market sentiment, or other factors that may be difficult or impossible to predict. Ciphex and its Ecosystem, including CPX Tokens, Digital Financings, and any current or future products, services, platforms, protocols, or autonomous systems, may be adversely affected by sudden market movements, flash crashes, liquidity withdrawals, widening bid-ask spreads, slippage, pricing anomalies, or disorderly market conditions. Autonomous or algorithmic systems may also be affected by model risk, data latency, signal distortion, execution delays, and structural market inefficiencies that may amplify losses during periods of elevated volatility. There can be no assurance that Ciphex, CPX Tokens, Digital Financings, or any current or future products, services, platforms, protocols, autonomous systems, analytical models, algorithms, or execution frameworks will successfully anticipate, adapt to, or mitigate the effects of extreme market volatility, liquidity disruptions, or adverse market conditions.

5.10 Fraud and Market Manipulation Risk

Digital asset markets, particularly those characterized by limited liquidity, fragmented trading venues, emerging infrastructure, or reduced regulatory oversight, may be vulnerable to fraud, market manipulation, abusive trading practices, and other unlawful conduct. Such conduct may include wash trading, spoofing, front-running, pump-and-dump schemes, coordinated trading activity, misleading information campaigns, market cornering, governance manipulation, liquidity pool exploitation, or other practices intended to distort market activity or pricing. Such activities may impair price discovery, increase volatility, reduce liquidity, adversely affect execution quality, diminish market confidence, or negatively affect the value, utility, adoption, or liquidity of CPX Tokens. In decentralized environments, the ability to identify, prevent, investigate, or remediate such conduct may be limited. There can be no assurance that Ciphex, its contributors, service providers, systems, or Ecosystem participants will identify, prevent, detect, investigate, or mitigate all fraudulent, manipulative, deceptive, abusive, or unlawful conduct that may affect the Ciphex Ecosystem, its products, services, Digital Financings, or related markets.

5.11 Financing and Growth Risk

Ciphex remains in an early stage of development and commercialization. Continued development, commercialization, expansion, and Ecosystem growth may require additional financing, technical resources, strategic partnerships, infrastructure, and operational support. Access to such resources may be affected by market conditions, investor demand, regulatory developments, competitive pressures, economic conditions, or the perceived commercial viability of the Ecosystem. Future financing activities may include corporate financing undertaken by Cipherion Capital SA ("Cipherion"), the parent legal entity of Ciphex, including without limitation, equity issuances, debt arrangements, strategic investments, commercial partnerships, and other capital-raising activities supporting the continued development and commercialization of Ciphex. Such activities may result in dilution of existing Cipherion shareholders or investors, changes to the tokenomics of the Ciphex Ecosystem, modifications to governance arrangements, preferential rights granted to future financing participants, or other outcomes that may adversely affect Cipherion, Ciphex, or CPX Token holders. There can be no assurance that Ciphex will obtain sufficient financing, strategic support, technical resources, commercial partnerships, or market adoption necessary to support the continued development, operation, commercialization, expansion, or long-term viability of the Ecosystem.

5.12 Privacy and Data Protection Risk

The Ciphex Ecosystem operates within blockchain-based and distributed technology environments that may involve public, immutable, and transparent transaction records. Transactions conducted on public blockchain networks may be permanently recorded and observable by third parties. Wallet addresses, transaction histories, network activity, and related metadata may be analyzed, correlated, monitored, or linked to individuals or entities through existing or future analytical methods. Ciphex may utilize third-party service providers, infrastructure providers, security vendors, analytics providers, communication platforms, and technology partners in connection with Ecosystem operations. Despite reasonable security measures, unauthorized access, data breaches, cybersecurity incidents, operational failures, human error, malicious activity, or technological developments may compromise the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of information. There can be no assurance that privacy measures, security controls, operational safeguards, or third-party protections will prevent unauthorized access, data breaches, loss of anonymity, re-identification, data correlation, surveillance activities, or other privacy-related risks.

5.13 Cybersecurity, Data Breach, and Access Risk

Proprietary information may be vulnerable to cyberattacks, data breaches, unauthorized access, malware, ransomware, phishing attacks, credential theft, system intrusions, infrastructure compromises, insider threats, or other cybersecurity incidents. Such events may result in the unauthorized acquisition, disclosure, copying, destruction, alteration, or misuse of intellectual property, trade secrets, confidential information, software, technical documentation, business processes, or other protected assets. Cybersecurity threats continue to evolve and may originate from individuals, organized criminal groups, state-sponsored actors, competitors, malicious insiders, or other third parties. There can be no assurance that security controls, access restrictions, monitoring systems, cybersecurity safeguards, or third-party service providers will prevent all unauthorized access, data breaches, theft, disclosure, or compromise of proprietary information or intellectual property.

5.14 Smart Contract Development Risk

The Ciphex Ecosystem may utilize smart contracts and blockchain-based software to automate protocol functions, transactions, token operations, and other Ecosystem activities. As software, smart contracts may contain vulnerabilities, coding defects, design flaws, logic errors, interoperability issues, security weaknesses, or other undiscovered defects. Such vulnerabilities may be exploited through malicious attacks, unauthorized access attempts, protocol manipulation, or other activities that may result in transaction failures, asset loss, service interruptions, incorrect execution outcomes, or impairment of Ecosystem functionality. Once deployed, smart contracts may be difficult, costly, delayed, or impossible to modify, reverse, remediate, or replace. There can be no assurance that any smart contract, blockchain application, protocol component, or related software utilized by Ciphex or third-party service providers will operate as intended or remain free from vulnerabilities, defects, exploits, or security risks.

5.15 Innovation and Competitive Market Risk

Ciphex operates in rapidly evolving, highly competitive markets. Advances in artificial intelligence, automation, blockchain technology, digital assets, tokenization, data analytics, decentralized finance, and financial technology may significantly alter competitive dynamics, user expectations, and market opportunities. Existing competitors and new market entrants may possess substantially greater financial resources, technical capabilities, regulatory advantages, proprietary technologies, brand recognition, distribution networks, or established user communities. The long-term success of Ciphex depends, in part, on its ability to maintain technological relevance, improve its products and services, adapt to changing market conditions, develop competitive technologies, attract Ecosystem participants, and respond effectively to evolving industry standards. There can be no assurance that Ciphex will successfully compete with existing or future competitors, maintain market differentiation, achieve meaningful adoption, or sustain a competitive position within the digital asset, tokenization, autonomous systems, or broader financial technology industries.

5.16 Operational and Infrastructure Risk

Ciphex, its Ecosystem, products, services, token utility features, and other future products, services, and token utility features, and strategic partnerships may rely on blockchain networks, validators, nodes, exchanges, cloud infrastructure providers, communication networks, software applications, market data providers, cybersecurity systems, hosting providers, and other third-party technologies and services. These components may experience outages, latency, congestion, software defects, cybersecurity incidents, operational failures, capacity limitations, or other disruptions. Such events may result in delayed transactions, reduced functionality, inaccurate outputs, interrupted services, degraded performance, settlement failures, restricted access, or operational losses.

Network upgrades, protocol changes, consensus failures, forks, infrastructure failures, or third-party service interruptions may also adversely affect Ecosystem operations. There can be no assurance that blockchain networks, infrastructure providers, exchanges, data providers, communication systems, software applications, or other third-party dependencies will remain continuously available, secure, reliable, or operational.

5.17 Expert Contributors, Creators, Founders, and Personnel Risk

The continued development, commercialization, operation, technical security, infrastructure, Ecosystem initiatives, and utility features of Ciphex depend, in part, upon the expertise and continued contributions of founders, creators, expert contributors, developers, researchers, engineers, contractors, and other specialized personnel (collectively, "Key Personnel"). The loss, unavailability, reduced participation, departure, incapacity, or replacement of Key Personnel may adversely affect development timelines, operational continuity, technical progress, commercialization initiatives, Ecosystem growth, regulatory preparedness, strategic execution, or the continued evolution of the Ecosystem. Competition for qualified personnel within the digital asset, artificial intelligence, blockchain, and financial technology industries may further increase these risks. There can be no assurance that Ciphex will retain, replace, or continue to have access to the expertise, services, knowledge, or contributions of Key Personnel or other specialized resources necessary to support the continued development, commercialization, operation, or long-term growth of the Ecosystem.

5.18 Intellectual Property Protection Risk

Ciphex relies upon proprietary intellectual property, confidential and trade secret information, research and development initiatives, quantitative and algorithmic frameworks, trade secrets, analytical methodologies, analytical and distributive logic models, technical overview architectures, infrastructure and software dependency layers, API functionalities, code components or integration assemblies, source code sampling integral to proprietary processes and methods for products and services, and any technical documentation containing descriptive, proprietary, or trade secret applications of the foregoing in connection with commercial and other economic strategies developed by Cipherion Capital SA ("Cipherion"), or by licensors, service providers, creators, or other contributors on behalf of Cipherion for the benefit and exclusive conditional use of Ciphex (collectively, the "Proprietary Properties").

Certain Proprietary Properties may not be eligible for patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret, contractual, or other legal protection in all jurisdictions. Proprietary Properties developed or licensed by Cipherion for the exclusive commercial benefit of Ciphex may be modified, withdrawn, reassigned, restricted, or otherwise become unavailable to Ciphex if Ciphex fails to achieve specified commercial objectives or satisfy other contractual conditions governing the use of such Proprietary Properties. Legal protections may also be limited, unavailable, unenforceable, costly to maintain, or subject to differing legal interpretations across jurisdictions. There can be no assurance that Ciphex will successfully protect, preserve, maintain, enforce, defend, or otherwise retain the benefit of its Proprietary Properties, intellectual property rights, trade secrets, confidential information, proprietary methodologies, software, systems, or other proprietary assets.

5.19 Corporate Theft and Malicious Actors

Ciphex may rely upon founders, creators, employees, contractors, advisors, Expert Contributors, consultants, service providers, strategic partners, and other third parties who may have access to Proprietary Properties in connection with their responsibilities. Such parties may unintentionally disclose, retain, copy, or transfer Proprietary Properties in connection with their responsibilities ("Accidental Exposure"), or intentionally retain, transfer, copy, modify, repurpose, exploit, or otherwise misappropriate Proprietary Properties, in whole or in part, for their own economic benefit or the benefit of others ("Malicious Actors"). Former personnel, service providers, developers, Expert Contributors, affiliates, professional service providers, or any individual or entity with access to Proprietary Properties in connection with their responsibilities may also contribute to Accidental Exposure or become Malicious Actors. Furthermore, competitors, business counterparties, or other third parties may seek to obtain Proprietary Properties through corporate or industrial espionage or other improper or unlawful means, including, without limitation, social engineering, recruitment efforts targeting current or former personnel, service providers, third-party developers, Expert Contributors, founders, creators, affiliates, professional service providers, or any other individual or entity with access to Proprietary Properties in connection with their responsibilities ("Corporate Espionage").

The compromise of Proprietary Properties through Accidental Exposure, Malicious Actors, or Corporate Espionage may directly or indirectly enable competing products or services, diminish the commercial value of Proprietary Properties, delay the development or commercialization of products, services, or Ecosystem initiatives, impair technology development, weaken Ciphex's strategic market position, reduce market confidence, adversely affect community engagement, or otherwise materially and adversely affect the commercial growth, competitiveness, expansion, or long-term value of the Ciphex Ecosystem. Ciphex implements confidentiality obligations, non-disclosure arrangements, technical safeguards, operational controls, physical security measures, and other contractual and administrative protections intended to safeguard Proprietary Properties. However, there can be no assurance that such measures will prevent all instances of Accidental Exposure, Malicious Actors, Corporate Espionage, unauthorized disclosure, misuse, breaches of confidentiality, or other misappropriation of Proprietary Properties.

5.20 Enforcement and Recovery Risk

Ciphex reserves the right, to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, to investigate and pursue, through civil, criminal, or other available legal proceedings, actual or suspected Malicious Actors, Corporate Espionage, unauthorized disclosure, misuse, breaches of confidentiality, or other misappropriation of Proprietary Properties. However, identifying such conduct may be difficult, delayed, costly, or impractical, particularly where activities involve anonymous actors, decentralized technologies, foreign jurisdictions, offshore entities, encrypted communications, or sophisticated methods intended to conceal misconduct. Enforcement efforts may require substantial time, expense, management attention, and legal resources. There can be no assurance that any investigation, litigation, arbitration, regulatory proceeding, injunctive action, settlement effort, or other enforcement activity will successfully identify responsible parties, prevent continuing misconduct, recover losses, protect Proprietary Properties, or otherwise achieve a favorable outcome.

5.21 Legal Remedies and Jurisdictional Limitations Risk

The availability and effectiveness of legal remedies may vary significantly across jurisdictions. Applicable statutes of limitation, evidentiary standards, jurisdictional requirements, conflicts of law, enforcement limitations, insolvency of responsible parties, procedural barriers, or other legal constraints may limit or prevent the availability of legal remedies. Even where intellectual property rights, confidentiality obligations, contractual protections, or trade secret protections relating to Proprietary Properties exist, enforcement may be delayed, restricted, economically impractical, or unsuccessful. Unauthorized use, disclosure, reproduction, commercialization, exploitation, or other misuse of Proprietary Properties may continue despite enforcement efforts. There can be no assurance that Ciphex will obtain timely, effective, or complete legal remedies, recover damages, prevent future violations, or fully mitigate the adverse effects of Malicious Actors, Corporate Espionage, unauthorized disclosure, misuse, breaches of confidentiality, or other misappropriation of Proprietary Properties.

5.22 Regulatory Change and Compliance Risk

The legal and regulatory frameworks applicable to digital assets, tokenized assets, blockchain technologies, autonomous systems, artificial intelligence, decentralized protocols, financial technology platforms, and related activities continue to evolve across many jurisdictions. Legislatures, regulators, courts, governmental agencies, self-regulatory organizations, and international standard-setting bodies may adopt, amend, interpret, or enforce laws, regulations, guidance, licensing requirements, reporting obligations, or supervisory frameworks that directly or indirectly affect Ciphex. Regulatory developments may increase compliance costs, impose additional licensing or reporting obligations, restrict operations, require modifications to products or services, limit market access, delay commercialization initiatives, or otherwise adversely affect the Ecosystem's development, operation, commercialization, or growth. There can be no assurance that future regulatory developments will not materially and adversely affect Ciphex, CPX Tokens, Digital Financings, or any current or future products, services, platforms, protocols, or Ecosystem activities.

5.23 Digital Asset and Tokenization Regulatory Risk

Autonomous digital market execution and digital asset management systems utilized by Ciphex Alpha, together with enterprise tokenization systems utilized by Atlas RWA Services, may be classified, regulated, or interpreted differently across jurisdictions. These interpretations may include, without limitation, Digital Financing supporting the development and commercialization of Ciphex, Ciphex Alpha, or Atlas RWA Services; the issuance, distribution, transfer, use, or tradability of CPX Tokens; the issuance of tokens for Atlas tokenization projects; the development of liquidity through decentralized or centralized digital asset exchanges; and secondary market activities undertaken by Atlas tokenization project owners. Regulatory treatment may evolve and may vary significantly between jurisdictions. Future laws, regulations, regulatory interpretations, or enforcement actions may restrict, prohibit, condition, delay, or otherwise affect the issuance, distribution, transfer, use, commercialization, tokenization, market accessibility, or secondary market activities relating to utility tokens, tokenized assets, or other digital assets associated with Ciphex, Ciphex Alpha, or Atlas RWA Services.

There can be no assurance that future regulatory developments will permit the continued development, commercialization, transferability, adoption, market availability, or lawful use of utility tokens, tokenized asset tokens, or tokens issued in connection with Digital Financings or other Ecosystem activities.

5.24 Autonomous Systems and Artificial Intelligence Regulatory Risk

Current or future products and services within Ciphex, including Ciphex Alpha and Atlas RWA Services, may utilize autonomous systems, algorithmic processes, quantitative models, analytical frameworks, artificial intelligence technologies, machine learning components, automated decision-making systems, or autonomous execution capabilities. Governments and regulatory authorities may adopt new requirements governing the design, operation, testing, oversight, transparency, explainability, accountability, monitoring, auditing, registration, certification, or deployment of autonomous systems and artificial intelligence technologies. Such requirements may increase development costs, impose operational restrictions, require system modifications, limit functionality, or delay the commercialization of products and services. There can be no assurance that autonomous systems, artificial intelligence technologies, analytical frameworks, or execution systems utilized within Ciphex will remain permissible, commercially viable, or capable of operating as originally designed under future legal or regulatory frameworks.

5.25 Licensing, Registration, and Authorization Risk

Regulatory authorities may determine that certain activities conducted within Ciphex may require registration, authorization, licensing, approval, notification, certification, permits, or other regulatory permissions in one or more jurisdictions. Such determinations may apply to all or portions of the Ecosystem, including digital asset activities, tokenization services, autonomous systems, software platforms, marketplace functions, commercial partnerships, Digital Financing, or current or future products and services. Obtaining, maintaining, modifying, or renewing such regulatory permissions may be costly, time-consuming, uncertain, or impractical. Regulatory approvals may be denied, delayed, restricted, conditioned, suspended, revoked, or subject to ongoing compliance obligations. There can be no assurance that any required registrations, licenses, approvals, authorizations, certifications, permits, or other regulatory permissions will be obtained, maintained, or renewed on commercially reasonable terms, if at all.

5.26 Commercialization and Market Adoption Risk

Regulatory developments may materially affect the ability of Ciphex to commercialize products and services, expand into new jurisdictions, attract participants, establish commercial partnerships, support tokenized assets, develop liquidity, or achieve broader market adoption. Compliance obligations may require significant capital expenditures, substantial operational, technical, legal, and compliance resources, and ongoing management attention. Regulatory uncertainty may discourage users, participants, counterparties, service providers, commercial partners, exchanges, financial institutions, or other stakeholders from engaging with Ciphex, Ciphex Alpha, Atlas RWA Services, CPX Tokens, tokenized asset projects, Digital Financings, or other Ecosystem activities. There can be no assurance that Ciphex will achieve successful commercialization, market acceptance, Ecosystem growth, commercial viability, or long-term expansion under existing or future legal or regulatory frameworks.

Regulatory developments may materially restrict, delay, impair, or prevent the continued development, operation, commercialization, adoption, or market accessibility of current or future Ciphex products, services, platforms, protocols, tokenization initiatives, autonomous systems, Digital Financings, or related Ecosystem activities.

5.27 Force Majeure and Natural Disaster Risk

Ciphex may be adversely affected by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, earthquakes, floods, hurricanes, wildfires, severe weather events, pandemics, public health emergencies, acts of war, terrorism, civil unrest, labor disruptions, governmental actions, infrastructure failures, power outages, telecommunications failures, internet disruptions, or other force majeure events. Such events may impair system availability, delay development activities, disrupt operations, damage infrastructure, restrict access to services, interrupt communications, or adversely affect Ecosystem participants and third-party service providers. There can be no assurance that Ciphex will remain unaffected by force majeure events or that such events will not materially and adversely affect Ecosystem operations, development activities, service availability, commercialization initiatives, or long-term growth.

Global Risk Acknowledgment

These Significant Risk Disclosures are intended to provide a comprehensive overview of significant risks associated with the Ciphex Capital Ecosystem ("Ciphex"), CPX Tokens, Digital Financings, and current or future products, services, platforms, token utility initiatives, enterprise tokenization initiatives, autonomous market systems, and related Ecosystem activities. They should not be regarded as a complete or exhaustive description of every risk that may arise. Additional risks and uncertainties, whether known or unknown, foreseeable or unforeseeable, may arise at any time and may materially and adversely affect Ciphex, its participants, or the value, availability, functionality, or commercial viability of Ecosystem products, services, Ciphex tokens, and its utility features. Participants should carefully consider these Significant Risk Disclosures together with all other disclosures, publications, policies, and related information made available by Ciphex before accessing, acquiring, holding, using, or participating in any Ecosystem activity.
